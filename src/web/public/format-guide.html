Value Format Guide

Tilewright automatically detects whether a value is a number or an ISO-8601 date/timestamp (such as Home Assistant's 2026-11-24T07:17:16+00:00) and applies your format string. Non-matching states like unavailable or home pass through untouched.

Numbers

Use 0 for required digits, # for optional digits, , for thousands separators, and . for decimals. Any text before or after the number pattern is kept as a literal prefix or suffix.

Dates & Times

Timestamps with a UTC offset (like +00:00 or Z) are automatically converted to your browser's local timezone — including automatic GMT / BST and DST adjustments for the exact date of the timestamp.
